## Why this file exists

`MASTER_PROJECT_BRIEF.md` §14A and every section of it headed "Inferred" carry a standing
stamp of the owner's own devising:

> **Provisional — ChatGPT 5.6 Sol contribution**

Beside it the brief proposes four labels for lifting that stamp — **Accepted** (adopted into
confirmed project direction), **Modified** (adopted after material revision), **Parked**
(retained for possible later use), **Rejected** (intentionally excluded) — and says that
until one is applied, every item stays provisional. Roughly four thousand lines of the brief
have been sitting under that stamp with no label applied to any of it.

`records/consolidation-register.md` is the first document that took the whole provisional
half seriously enough to argue with it row by row, and on 2026-09-04 the owner triaged it.
So the labels finally have something to be applied to. **The brief itself is read-only** —
it is rationale and never build authority (blueprint 6.0 §0.1.6), and nothing in this
consolidation writes to it. This file is where the labels live instead, and it is the record
a later reader should look at before treating any part of the brief's inferred half as
still unlabelled.

### The four subsets, so "Accepted" is not read as "built"

Accepted and built are different things, and conflating them is how a backlog turns into a
claim. Within the labelled set:

- **In the 6.0 scope: one hundred and thirty rows.** Built in this version, to
  `blueprint_TGTL_6.0.md`, in the batches recorded in `DECISIONS.md` §8.
- **Deferred to the 6.1 evidence pass: seventeen rows.** Accepted, not built, not partly
  built, and listed in `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md` §0.B by number.
- **Parked as areas: eight.** The social manual (N-141), the Atlas (N-154), the story
  campaign "The Years Between" (N-220), the Living Scene (N-250), the ethics and meaning
  wing (N-393), the topic set (N-415, N-421, N-422, N-424, N-425 — counted here as one area
  decision), archetype resemblance (N-086), and the sex-lens research batch (N-380). Each is
  a version of its own; nothing inside any of them was harvested piecemeal. They are named
  in `WHATS_COMING` in the owner's own research-priority order (N-437).
- **Stop-and-ask: three.** N-324 frame-strength tags, N-376 the peer-advantage population
  rule, N-381 the screening-threshold record kind. Each would *add* a lint rule, which is
  never done without the owner saying the word. Parked, and listed in
  `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md` §0.C.

### What is NOT labelled by this file

- **The brief's non-inferred half.** Everything the owner wrote directly was never
  provisional and needs no label.
- **Anything the register did not reach.** The register covers what eleven sweeps found in
  the nine prototypes, the four specs and the brief. A part of the brief's inferred half
  that generated no candidate row is still unlabelled, and this file does not pretend
  otherwise. The honest statement is: every inferred item that produced a register row now
  carries a label, and the register is the map of which those were.
- **Future contributions.** The stamp still applies to anything added under it from here.
  The labels are applied to a set of rows on a date, not granted to a source.
