# The content pipeline record (blueprint 4.0 §9.5)

Content at this scale ships only through the pipeline: schema-constrained authors
in parallel batches, an **adversarial** verifier per batch instructed to find
violations rather than to approve, and a repair pass against every blocking
finding. This is what each batch was checked against and what the checks found.

Every batch was verified against, at minimum: the crisis-tier boundary, the
loss-tier boundary, the §5.1 capacity-event boundary, the never-command five, the
no-numbers rule, the no-reader-content rule, the no-dominant-option rule, the
endurance/supportLink rule, the reserved `calibrated` evidence label, the schema,
and the voice.
